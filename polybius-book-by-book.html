How much of this is solid. Chapter numbers follow the Loeb/Büttner-Wobst division. For Books 1–6, which survive continuously, I spot-checked the anchors against Paton's text (Thayer's LacusCurtius transcription of the 1922 Loeb) and they hold. For Books 7–40 the "chapters" are really the numbering of excerpted fragments, not a continuous narrative, so a mark's position on the rail shows roughly where the excerptors placed it, not where the episode fell in Polybius's own paragraph count. Marks I could not pin confidently are flagged approx. in the readout. Books 17 and 19 have no rail because no text survives; 37 has almost none.

The colours are a simplification. One or two "powers" per episode is a crude reading of a historian whose whole point is that everybody is entangled with everybody. Numidians, Illyrians and Iberians are lumped together for want of hues, which Polybius would have thought sloppy, and I flag it rather than hide it.
