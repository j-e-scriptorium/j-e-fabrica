About

St. Isidore University Workshop, formally Jsidoris Euniversitatis Fabrica ("JEF" for short), is the workshop of the faculty of St. Isidore University, located in far northern California, although not to be found on most maps. Where the Press sets type, the Workshop keeps the lathes: visualizations, games, and the odd contraption that does not fit on a page.

The Workshop currently shares a single faculty roster with the Press; a workshop-specific appointment or two may follow as the bench fills up.

  • Ludovico Ambrosius is professor of bee-keeping and board game design.
  • Jørgen Agosto Comgall is professor of the philosophy of the history of philosophy of history.
  • E. Lawrence Hayward is professor of regularity.
  • Lyzander Keretzky is lecturer in noneuclidean architecture.
  • Edkar Marenko is instructor of industry.
  • Porphyry Pteropsichore is professor of order and color.
  • Hugo Purcell is professor of psychometry and physiology.
  • Michael A. Seeley is lecturer in systemic history.
  • Tom G. Turner is professor of dialects and dialectics.
