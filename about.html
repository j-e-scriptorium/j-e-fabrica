About

St. Isidore University Workshop, formally Jsidoris Euniversitatis Fabrica ("JEF" for short), is the workshop of the faculty of St. Isidore University, located in far northern California, although not to be found on most maps. Where the Press sets type, the Workshop keeps the lathes: visualizations, games, and the odd contraption that does not fit on a page.

The Workshop's faculty roster overlaps with the Press's rather than duplicating it; a workshop-specific appointment or two may follow as the bench fills up.

  • Ludovico Ambrosius is professor of bee-keeping and board game design; he and S. Minerva Scribner collaborated on School.
  • Edkar Marenko is instructor of industry; he and C. Persephone collaborated on Many Rooms.
  • C. Persephone is professor of apotheosis; she and Edkar Marenko collaborated on Many Rooms.
  • S. Minerva Scribner, apprentice, collaborated with Ludovico Ambrosius on School.
  • Michael A. Seeley is lecturer in systemic history, and made Strata and The Expansion of America.
  • Tom G. Turner is professor of dialects and dialectics, and made Twenty Voyages.

The pseudonym of the chief clerk of JEF, as of JES, is Salvator R. Tarnmoor. He sporadically checks his email address, but a more reliable point of contact would be @s_r_tarnmoor on X.
