The Fabrica, formally Jsidoris Euniversitatis Fabrica ("JEF" for short), is the workshop of the faculty of St. Isidore University — sister house to the Press, and equally hard to find on a map.

Where the Press binds pages, the Workshop builds things that move: visualizations that chart voyages and histories, and recreations — games old and new, mechanized for a screen.
