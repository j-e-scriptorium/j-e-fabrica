Lignum Vitae [WIP]

a duel of growing trees

Two mages face each other across a field, and neither casts. Each tends a tree that grows on its own — bend it into barrels and fans, graft its tips into rings that ring the charge louder, and fire by cutting a limb off. A campaign of five bouts against better and better opponents, and the tree you shape in the first is the tree you fight the last with.
