Many Rooms

No name, no date — not even always fourteen lines. Guess the year the sonnet was written.

"Written" means the year of first publication — except when a sonnet was first published after its poet's death, in which case it means the year it was composed (marked "composed" on the reveal screen; published-in-lifetime sonnets are marked "published"). A few dates are only approximate, marked accordingly.
