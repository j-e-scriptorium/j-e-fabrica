a sonnet-dating game

Strata Open

a where-and-when game

Given only a person, place, event, or idea from the ancient world — no name attached — drop a pin and set a year, in the style of Anthropeum's Geoguessr. 379 rounds drawn from The Story of the World, vol. 1, scored on both place and time at once.
