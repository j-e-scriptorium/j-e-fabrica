Reading the map

State outlines are modern borders throughout — this map does not attempt to draw the precise, often short-lived boundaries of historical territories (Missouri Territory, Indian Territory, and the rest), only when each present-day state's land became American ground and when it became a state. Each state is assigned to the single acquisition its land is dominantly identified with; land genuinely contested between two acquisitions, or reorganized more than once, is simplified to one. Boundaries come from the US Census Bureau (via us-atlas), Albers USA projection.

State origin blurbs, illustrations, and flags are illustrative, not exhaustive — see each state's own history for the fuller story.
