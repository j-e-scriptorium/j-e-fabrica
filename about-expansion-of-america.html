The Expansion of America

A map growing outward: territory added to the United States, year by year, and the states admitted into it.

By Michael A. Seeley.

Scrub the year from 1776 to 1959 and watch two things happen at different speeds: the country's territory arriving — by treaty, purchase, annexation, or cession — and its states being admitted into the union, which is very often a much later event than the territory itself becoming American ground. The map shades the two differently: tan for territory that is American but not yet a state, darker for statehood, so the gap between the two is visible rather than smoothed over.

Hover a state to name it; click one for its own short account of the path to statehood, and the timeline jumps to the year that state was admitted. Every state's entry comes from a single reference source pairing all fifty with a paragraph on how each got there, ranked by how interesting a story it tells.
