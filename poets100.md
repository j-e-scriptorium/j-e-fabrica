## Summary
Of ~100 canonical poets: ~42 wrote no meaningful sonnets (the form's
history is genuinely lumpy); 47 authors now in corpus. Remaining ●-grade
gaps, ranked: Herbert's Temple sonnets · Clare's mass · full Elegiac
Sonnets · Keats's last four famous ones · Wilde · Swinburne · Dunbar ·
Blake's Evening Star · Hunt's Nile · Raleigh's one · Wroth & Drummond
(need non-PG sources) · the four unmirrored 1920s volumes (Auden, Frost
1928, Millay 1928, cummings). Everything else is ○ optional texture.
